🖨️ Printer Settings

Select your printer type. Thermal printers use narrower widths (2", 3", 4"). Standard is for A4/Letter paper.

Prints directly to your default printer without showing the print dialog. Requires launching Chrome with --kiosk-printing flag. Create a shortcut: chrome.exe --kiosk-printing

🧾 KOT auto-print

Every order prints a kitchen ticket (KOT) on the counter PC. Keep the Print Station page open there, in Chrome started with --kiosk-printing so it prints without a dialog.

🥡 Takeaway & Parcel

Put the takeaway QR at the counter. Customers order with their name and number, get a token, and see when it's ready. Staff handle these on Take Order → Takeaway.

Added once per takeaway order, on top of each dish's own packing charge (set on the Menu page). Packing is taxed at the same GST rate as the food.

⭐ Loyalty Points

Customers earn points on what they pay (by mobile number, entered on the bill) and use them at the counter as a discount. Points come off before tax. See every customer on the Customers page.

Customers get back about {{ loyaltyCashbackPercent }}% of what they spend.

0 = no limit. Stops staff collecting points on their own number.
Given on a customer's first bill. 0 = off.
On a bill within 3 days of their birthday, once a year. The bill screen asks for the date. 0 = off.

🏨 Hotel

Adds Rooms (check-in, room bills, checkout) and Room Service (delivery queue) to the sidebar, and lets checked-in guests order from the QR in their room. Add Front Desk and Room Service staff on the Staff page.

🎰 Lucky Draw Discount

Customers can try a lucky draw for a discount after placing an order. The discount is applied directly to their bill. The lucky draw only appears for orders placed by the customer (not by waiter).

Turn the lucky draw feature on or off for customers.

Lucky draw options will range from 0% up to this value. Eg: 10 → options like 3%, 5%, 10%, ₹25 off, etc.
