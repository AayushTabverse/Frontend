🎰 Lucky Draw Discount

Customers can try a lucky draw for a discount after placing an order. The discount is applied directly to their bill. The lucky draw only appears for orders placed by the customer (not by waiter).

Turn the lucky draw feature on or off for customers.

Lucky draw options will range from 0% up to this value. Eg: 10 → options like 3%, 5%, 10%, ₹25 off, etc.
